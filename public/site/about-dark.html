I’m a Ukrainian designer with 4 years of experience in graphic design, web design and no-code development.

I like friendly but professional communication. I believe that client and designer should work as equals, listen to each other and keep the process comfortable.

I create websites that help people quickly understand your brand: who you are, what you do and why they should choose you. For me, a website should not only look beautiful — it should feel clear, comfortable and easy to use.

I care about structure, visual details, mood and brand personality. Every project has its own story, so I don’t believe in one-size-fits-all design.
